🧠 Copy Prompt Box

Help me write a calm, empathetic response to a negative or hurtful online comment.

Use the EFL method: Empower ✊, Flip 🔄, Light 💡.

1. Empower: centre compassion, not confrontation.
2. Flip: turn tension into understanding or awareness.
3. Light: end with hope or encouragement.

Keep it short, human, and constructive.

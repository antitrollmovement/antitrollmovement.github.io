💫 The Five Frames

Each frame represents a value — a way to show who you are and what you stand for. They’re not filters. They’re symbols of empathy, courage, and light.

💛 Ally

For those who show kindness, speak light, and stand up for others — even quietly.

🔄 Ambassador

For the ones flipping negativity into awareness, truth, and reflection.

🧠 Thought Leader

For those who guide others with perspective, knowledge, and emotional depth.

🔥 Survivor

For the voices that kept going when it would’ve been easier to disappear.

🧱 Architect

For the builders — those creating safer spaces, stronger systems, and better worlds.

Every frame is a statement. Not about who you are online — but what kind of energy you leave behind. 💡
